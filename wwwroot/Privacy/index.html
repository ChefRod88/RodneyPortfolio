Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Overview

RC DEV LLC ("we", "us", or "our"), operated by Rodney A. Chery, is committed to safeguarding the privacy of visitors to https://www.rodneyachery.com. This Privacy Policy describes how information is collected, used, and protected when you interact with this website.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when using our contact, quote, or technical support forms:

  • Contact & Identification: Name, business name, and email address.
  • Project Information: Inquiries, budget parameters, project scopes, or troubleshooting descriptions.
  • Technical Telemetry: Anonymized usage data collected through Google Analytics (GA4) and security verification headers provided by Cloudflare.

3. How We Use Information

The information collected is strictly utilized to:

  • Respond directly to your project inquiries, consultation requests, or bug reports.
  • Evaluate software project feasibility, prepare formal proposals, and provide ongoing technical support.
  • Maintain website security, mitigate spam submissions, and ensure high operational availability.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

4. Third-Party Services & Security

This website employs trusted third-party technical providers:

  • Cloudflare: Edge content delivery, SSL/TLS encryption, and Turnstile automated bot protection.
  • Google Analytics (GA4): Aggregated site traffic analysis with IP anonymization enabled.
  • Resend: Secure transactional email dispatch for form inquiries.

5. Contacting Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to request the removal of your inquiry data, please contact us directly at rodney@globalrcdev.com.
